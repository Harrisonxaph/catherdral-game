INSTALL VESPER

Save VESPER to your Home Screen and play like a standalone game.

  1. Open the game in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen or Install app in your browser menu.
  3. Tap the VESPER icon to play.

For offline play, open the game online at least once and wait for “Offline ready” on the title screen. Your best run is stored on this device.
